# PayamaX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

PayamaX (`no1.payamax`) is an Android app that filters and organizes SMS messages on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your phone, and what is sent only when you choose to send it.

## Information used on your device

**SMS messages.** PayamaX requests permission to read SMS so it can show, search, and classify messages as useful or spam. Classification runs on your device. Your inbox is not uploaded during normal use.

**Contacts.** PayamaX requests permission to read contacts so it can tell whether a sender is already in your phone book. Your contact list is not uploaded.

**Network access.** The app uses an internet connection to send a report when you submit one, and to tell whether the device is online.

**Storage.** If you export messages to Excel, the file is written to your Downloads folder on this device. On older Android versions the app may ask for storage permission to do that.

## Reports you choose to send

PayamaX does not send message contents to our servers unless you submit a report.

A report may include:

- the sender address or number
- the time the message was received
- your comment
- the app’s classification and your classification
- a SHA-512 hash of the message text
- the message text, only if you allow it

For spam, the full text and sender are included only when you turn on that option. For other messages, you can send the hash only, or include the full text. Reports are stored on your device and sent to PayamaX at `https://api.pax.no1.ir`. We use them to improve spam detection.

## Crash reports

PayamaX uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. If the app crashes, Crashlytics may receive a crash log and device details such as the device model, Android version, and app version. This information is used to find and fix bugs. It does not include your SMS messages or contacts.

Google’s privacy policy is at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Firebase information is described at [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Sharing a message

If you use the share action, Android opens the app you pick. What that app receives is handled by that app, not by this policy.

## What we do not do

PayamaX does not require an account. It does not show ads. It does not sell your information. It does not use your messages or contacts for advertising.

## How long information is kept

Messages and contacts remain in the Android apps that store them. PayamaX keeps local classification data and saved reports on the device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Reports you submit are kept on our servers while they are useful for improving the filter. You can ask us to delete a report you sent.

If Android backup is turned on for this phone, Android may back up app data as part of the device backup.

## Children

PayamaX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
